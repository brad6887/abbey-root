# OmeletYouFinish.com Exhibit Refresh

## Objective

Refresh the OmeletYouFinish.com exhibit so it preserves the failed
domain-purchase story while making clear that Brad genuinely loves omelets.

## Definition of Done

- The existing `MDI-0001` exhibit retains its museum organization, visual
  style, deadpan tone, and central bad-domain-purchase premise.
- The narrative states Brad's preference for simple, well-made omelets without
  pancake batter or gimmicks.
- The exhibit records Waffle House as the standard and distinguishes proper
  omelets from overcooked hotel and cruise-ship buffet eggs.
- Three supplied real omelet photographs and the supplied fictional-musicians
  promotional image appear with meaningful filenames, alt text, and captions.
- The Astro site builds successfully and the four published image paths are
  present in the generated site.
- A session update captures the work.
- Brad reviews the complete status and diff before any commit.

## Summary

The exhibit now separates the quality of the omelet philosophy from the
quality of the domain-purchase decision. It adds Brad's straightforward
standard for a good omelet, expands the museum plaque and narrative without
discarding the original joke, and prepares a four-item photographic-evidence
gallery using the Museum's existing artifact presentation.

## Accomplishments

- Reviewed the project status, Abbey session workflow, current exhibit, and
  existing Museum photographic-gallery pattern.
- Preserved the original accession number, layout, ratings, and failed-domain
  premise.
- Added Brad's omelet philosophy and Waffle House standard in the exhibit's
  established voice.
- Added captions and accessible descriptions for three real omelet photographs
  and one fictional-musicians promotional image.
- Imported all four images under stable, descriptive Museum filenames and
  removed embedded photograph metadata before publication.

## Impact

OmeletYouFinish.com remains a bad idea worthy of the Museum, but the refreshed
exhibit no longer implies that Brad's enthusiasm for omelets was the mistake.

## Lessons Learned

in a continued Codex task. Once the originals are available, publishing copies
should also remove embedded device and location metadata.
