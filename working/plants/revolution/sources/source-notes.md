# Revolution Source Notes

- The owner’s rescue history and narrative supplied in the July 2026
  Revolution webpage conversation.
- The 35 Revolution photographs and matching XMP sidecars supplied in
  `/home/bcooke/incoming/photos`.

  lost interest after the flowers began dropping.

  later.
- All photographs before January 5, 2026 belong to the pre-tracking history.
- The original nursery plug was discovered and removed during the June 13,
  2026 repot.
- Representative photographs should be published instead of every supplied
  angle.
