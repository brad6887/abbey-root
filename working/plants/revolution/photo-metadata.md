# Metadata Sources

- Adobe XMP sidecars supplied with all source photographs.

- Visual review of the full source set.

# Publication Choices

- Photos before January 5, 2026 are grouped into one Before Tracking entry.
- Photos 1, 2, and 3 represent the pre-tracking period without publishing every
  older angle.
- Repetitive same-day views were omitted in favor of the clearest overall or
  detail image.
- Photo 21 is the hero because it best shows the full 2026 bloom.
- Photo 35 is the current image because it is the newest supplied photograph.
- HEIC source photos 1 and 2 were rendered as PNG files for browser-compatible
  publication. The original files and XMP sidecars remain unchanged.

# Notes

- The exact rescue date is not documented.
- Revolution moved from Texas to Florida and returned to Texas about a year
  later; exact move dates are not assigned to the older photos.
- The source images and sidecars in incoming/photos were not modified.
