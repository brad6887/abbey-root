### Observations

These are representative photos from Revolution’s early years in the
collection, dating from October 2020 through April 2025. Detailed tracking had
not begun, but the photos document her continued growth and flowering through
multiple homes and growing environments.

### Observations

system that had filled the clear inner pot and extended well beyond it.
Repotting would have to wait until flowering finished.

## 2026-06-13 — Repot Day

### Photos

- Revolution - 28.jpg
- Revolution - 29.jpg

### Observations

The roots had filled the clear pot and wrapped tightly through the old,
moisture-retentive material. The original nursery plug was still buried inside
the root system. It was removed with the deteriorated material before
Revolution was placed into a fresh, more open bark mix.
