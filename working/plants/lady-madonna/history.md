# Lady Madonna

## Before 2026-01-05 — Rescue Before Tracking

### What Happened

I acquired Lady Madonna a few months before I began tracking her. I recall that
she was close to dead at the time.

### Notes

- The exact acquisition date is not documented.
- I recall that she was probably a $5 Lowe's clearance orchid that I purchased
  in late 2025. The source files do not independently confirm the retailer,
  price, or date.
