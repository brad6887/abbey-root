# Publication Choices

  original bloom display and the reason Honey Pie stood out.
- The July 26 full-plant photograph is the current image because it shows both
  the remaining flowers and the normally aging bottom leaf.
- Twelve photographs provide a clear weekly progression across overall
  condition, flowers, roots, crown, and the oldest leaf.
- Photographs 8 and 12 are retained privately because stronger views from the
  same dates already document their subjects.
