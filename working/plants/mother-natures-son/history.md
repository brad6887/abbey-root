## 2026-04-12 — Root Cleanup and Repot

### Photos

- Mother_natures_son - 16.jpeg
- Mother_natures_son - 17.jpeg
- Mother_natures_son - 18.jpeg
- Mother_natures_son - 19.jpeg
- Mother_natures_son - 20.jpeg
- Mother_natures_son - 21.jpeg
- Mother_natures_son - 22.jpeg

### What Happened

Removed the plant from its old setup, inspected the root system, removed dead
brown roots, and repotted it into fresh open medium.

### Observations

- The old root system was compromised.
- Brown, dead roots required cleanup.
- The new pot contained coarse bark, charcoal, and perlite.
- The active basal growth remained intact through the repot.

### Lessons

The plant needed a clean, airy setup, but the living growths and viable roots
had to be preserved rather than stripped indiscriminately.

## 2026-07-26 — New Root From the Active Lead

### Photos

- Mother_natures_son - 33.JPG

### What Happened

A bright white root with a green growing tip emerged from the new lead.

### Observations

- The new lead had elongated toward becoming a pseudobulb.
- The lead was beginning to establish its own root system.
- Mature growths remained green and functional.
- The bark remained open, with charcoal and perlite visible.
- The pot drained well after watering.

### Lessons

The new root marked the transition from recovery supported by old pseudobulbs
to self-sustaining new growth. Mother Nature's Son had accepted the repot and
was actively building the next part of the plant.
