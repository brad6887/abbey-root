# Revolution Plant Publication

## Summary

Revolution now has a complete Orchid Rescue profile covering her early
pre-tracking years, 2026 bloom, June repot, and summer recovery.

## Accomplishments

- Built and validated Revolution’s Plant Model workspace.
- Selected 26 representative photographs from the 35-photo source set.
- Grouped all photographs before January 5, 2026 under Before Tracking.
- Preserved the two oldest HEIC photos as browser-compatible PNG files.
- Added Coming Soon entries for Something, Golden Slumbers, and Martha My Dear.
- Generated and successfully built the complete site.

## Lessons Learned

- The strongest-photo approach keeps the timeline complete without publishing
  every repetitive same-day angle.
- Historical HEIC images can be retained without changing the incoming source
  files.

## Next Steps
