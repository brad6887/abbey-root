# Plant Website Update Runbook

## Summary

The first real multi-plant update proved the complete path from iPhone captions
through renamed originals, a reviewed worksheet, canonical history updates,
sanitized publishing, and a successful site build. That procedure now lives in
one plant website update runbook.

## Accomplishments

- Documented individual and multi-plant updates.
- Documented manual species, date, and history corrections.
- Preserved the boundary between canonical plant workspaces and generated site
  output.
- Included validation, publication, visual review, and session completion.
- Added recovery rules based on the issues found during real use.

## Lessons Learned

- A workflow becomes reusable when its exceptions and safety boundaries are as
  clear as its normal commands.
- Real use produced the details that a speculative runbook would have missed.

## Next Steps

- Follow the runbook during the next plant update and refine it from observed
  workflow changes rather than anticipation.
