# Phal McCartney Joins the Orchid Rescue

## Summary

Phal McCartney now has a complete Orchid Rescue profile.

The timeline begins in January with two growing flower spikes, follows the
purple-and-white bloom through April, and resumes after a month packed in a
plastic tub during the move from Florida to Texas. By late July, the flowers
were long gone, but a new center leaf had broadened and matured.

## Accomplishments

- Preserved all 23 photographs and their available metadata.
- Published the complete supplied story and dated photo timeline.
- Used photo 20 as the hero image.
- Replaced the original coming-soon profile with the full thriving-plant page.

## Lessons Learned

- A plant can survive an inconvenient tour delay surprisingly well.
- Approximate history is still useful when it is labeled honestly instead of
  converted into a falsely precise date.
