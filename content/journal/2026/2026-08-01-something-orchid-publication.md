# Something Orchid Publication

## Summary

Something now has a complete Orchid Rescue profile covering her January
clearance-rack rescue from Publix in Naples, the move from Florida to Texas,
and a second recovery from severe dehydration.

## Accomplishments

- Built and validated Something's canonical Plant Model workspace.
- Published all 21 supplied photographs as a dated recovery timeline.
- Preserved the conversational rescue narrative and slightly goofy captions.
- Used XMP sidecars to recover dates missing from several exported images.
- Replaced the coming-soon placeholder with a generated public page.
- Added current and index imagery through canonical plant metadata so the orchid matrix remains derived.

## Lessons Learned

- XMP sidecars remain useful provenance when exported photographs lose their embedded capture dates.
- Grouping multiple views from the same event keeps a complete photo record readable without duplicating captions.
